### Repeat-Resident Gene Curation (RL2 and RS1)
The repeat-resident genes RL2 (ICP0) and RS1 (ICP4) showed elevated sequence failure rates due to real clinical indels and tandem repeat length variation in GC-rich domains (70-100% of failures classified as REAL_INDEL by nucleotide-level alignment). Clean sequence counts were RL2: 299 (HSV-1), 98 (HSV-2); RS1: 241 (HSV-1), 59 (HSV-2). The reduced HSV-2 RS1 count reflects documented assembly variability in the ICP4 repeat region and is reported as a limitation.
